Settings

This app runs entirely on your device. To enable photo scanning & AI analysis, connect your own Gemini API key below.

Gemini API key

Get a key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It is saved only in this browser's local storage on this device — it is never sent anywhere except directly to Google's Gemini API, for extraction, generic-name matching, DRP analysis, interaction checking, and clinical discussion generation. Anyone with access to this device/browser could read it from local storage, so don't use this on a shared or public device.

How data is stored

Patient cases, labs, meds and notes you enter or verify are saved locally on this device (browser local storage) — not to any Google or third-party server. Uninstalling the browser app or clearing site data will erase them, so export/print important cases from the Reports tab. Use anonymous Case IDs and avoid entering direct patient identifiers.
